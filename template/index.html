            How the numbers are made

            Measured burn = average logged intake minus the change in trend weight (3,500 calories per pound), using only fully logged days. Unlogged snacks or weekends make both intake and measured burn look lower. Garmin's number is its daily total-burn estimate. Fat and lean mass come from the scale's body-fat reading, which moves with hydration, so trust the 7-day averages over any single day.
